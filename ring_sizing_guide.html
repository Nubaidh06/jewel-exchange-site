Printable Ring Sizing Guide

How to Use This Guide

  1. Print at 100% Scale: Ensure your printer settings are set to "100%" or "Actual Size". Do not select "Fit to Page" or "Scale to Fit".
  2. Verify the Scale: Place a standard credit card in the box below. It should fit perfectly within the gold lines. If not, reprint the page at the correct scale.
  3. Measure Your Ring: Find a ring that currently fits the intended finger perfectly.
  4. Match the Size: Place your ring over the circles below. Match the inside edge of your ring to the black circle. When the black circle is just barely touching the inside of your ring, you have found your correct size.
  5. Between Sizes? We recommend choosing the larger size for maximum comfort.
